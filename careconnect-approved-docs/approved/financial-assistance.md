# Financial Assistance and Payment Support (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Who can apply
Financial assistance (sometimes called charity care) is available to patients who meet income and eligibility criteria. Uninsured and underinsured patients are encouraged to apply.

## What it can cover
Depending on eligibility, assistance may reduce or, in some cases, fully cover the cost of medically necessary care. It does not cover purely cosmetic or non-covered services.

## How to apply
Request a financial-assistance application from the billing office or download it from the Riverside Health website. You will typically be asked for proof of income and household information.

## Payment plans
If you do not qualify for assistance, or while your application is reviewed, you can set up an interest-free payment plan to spread the balance over time.

## Deadlines
Apply as early as possible. You can usually apply even after receiving a bill; the billing office will explain any time limits that apply to your account.

## Help completing the form
If you need help understanding or completing the application, the billing office and financial counsellors can assist, including with interpreter support.
