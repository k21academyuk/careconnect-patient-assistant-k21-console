# Getting Your Lab and Test Results (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Where to find results
Most lab and imaging results appear in the patient portal once they are ready and reviewed. You will receive a notification when new results are available.

## How long results take
Routine blood tests are often available within 1–3 days. Imaging and specialised tests can take longer because they are reviewed by a specialist before release.

## Understanding your results
The portal shows your results and reference ranges. It does not interpret what your results mean for you personally. Your clinician will explain your results and any next steps — what a result means for your health is a clinical decision, not something the portal or support staff can advise on.

## Abnormal or urgent results
If a result needs prompt attention, your care team will contact you directly. If you are worried about a symptom while waiting for results, contact your care team; for emergency symptoms, seek emergency care.

## Results for someone else
To access results on behalf of another person (for example, a child or a relative you care for), you must have the proper authorisation on file. See the Medical Records Request guide.
