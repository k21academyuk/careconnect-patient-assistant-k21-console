# Interpreter and Accessibility Services (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Language interpreters
Free interpreter services, including sign-language interpreters, are available for appointments. Request an interpreter when booking so one can be arranged. Phone interpreting can be used at short notice.

## Written information in other formats
Key information can be provided in large print, easy-read, or other languages on request. Ask reception or your care team.

## Mobility access
The hospital is wheelchair accessible, with accessible parking, entrances, lifts, and toilets. Wheelchairs are available to borrow near the main entrance.

## Hearing and vision support
Hearing loops are available at many reception desks. Tell staff if you use a hearing aid or need information read aloud. Assistance animals are welcome.

## Additional needs
If you have autism, a learning disability, dementia, or another condition that affects your visit, tell the team in advance so reasonable adjustments — such as a quieter waiting area or a longer appointment — can be arranged where possible.

## Carers
Carers are welcome to support you during your visit. Let staff know if someone is attending as your carer.
