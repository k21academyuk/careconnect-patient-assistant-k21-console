# Pharmacy Services (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Hours and location
The outpatient pharmacy is on the ground floor near the main entrance. Typical hours are 08:00–20:00 on weekdays and 09:00–17:00 at weekends. Hours may change on public holidays — check notices at the pharmacy counter.

## Collecting medication
Bring your photo ID when collecting medicines. Someone else can collect on your behalf if they bring your details and their own ID, where allowed.

## Transferring a prescription
You can ask to transfer a prescription to or from another pharmacy. The pharmacy team will handle the transfer; allow extra time for controlled medicines.

## Mail and home delivery
Home delivery may be available for eligible patients and medicines. Ask the pharmacy team whether your prescription qualifies.

## Controlled substances
Controlled medicines follow stricter legal rules. They may require collection in person, valid ID, and may not be refilled early. The pharmacy team will explain the specific requirements.

## Medicine questions
Pharmacists can explain how to store a medicine, general information printed on the label, and collection logistics. Questions about whether a medicine is right for you, or about changing a dose, are clinical decisions handled by a licensed clinician.
