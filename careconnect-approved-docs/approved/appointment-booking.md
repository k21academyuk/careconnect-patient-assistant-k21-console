# Booking an Appointment (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Ways to book
You can book an appointment through the Riverside Health patient portal, by phone with the scheduling desk, or in person at reception. Online booking is available 24/7 for many appointment types.

## New patients
If this is your first visit, please register first (see the Patient Portal guide) and arrive 15 minutes early to complete registration. Bring your photo ID and insurance card.

## Returning patients
Returning patients can book directly through the portal. Your saved details make booking faster.

## Referrals
Some specialist appointments require a referral from your primary care provider. If a referral is needed, the specialist's office cannot book you until the referral is received. See the Referrals and Specialist Care guide for details.

## Choosing a time
When booking, you will see available dates and times. If nothing suitable is shown, join the cancellation waitlist through the portal to be offered earlier slots when they open.

## Reminders
You will receive appointment reminders by email or text if you have opted in. You can change your reminder preferences in the portal.

## Interpreters and accessibility
If you need an interpreter, wheelchair access, or other support, request it when booking so it can be arranged in advance. See the Interpreter and Accessibility Services guide.
