# Telehealth and Virtual Visits (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## What telehealth is
A virtual visit lets you see a clinician by secure video for suitable appointments, such as follow-ups and some routine consultations. Not every visit can be done virtually; your care team will advise if an in-person visit is needed.

## What you need
A smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera and microphone, a stable internet connection, and the patient portal or the visit link sent to you. Find a quiet, private, well-lit space.

## Joining your visit
Sign in to the portal a few minutes early and select your virtual appointment, or use the link in your reminder. You may enter a virtual waiting room until the clinician joins.

## If technology fails
If the connection drops, rejoin using the same link. If you cannot reconnect, the clinician's office will contact you by phone, or you can call the scheduling desk.

## When telehealth is not appropriate
Telehealth is not for emergencies. For urgent or emergency symptoms, seek urgent or emergency care rather than booking a virtual visit.

## Privacy
Virtual visits use a secure, encrypted connection. Join from a private place, as you would for an in-person visit.
