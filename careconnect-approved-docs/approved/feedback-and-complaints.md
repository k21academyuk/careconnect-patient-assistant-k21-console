# Feedback, Compliments, and Complaints (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## We want to hear from you
Your feedback helps improve care. You can share a compliment, a suggestion, or a concern at any time.

## How to give feedback
Speak to a member of staff or the ward manager, use a feedback form in the department, submit feedback through the patient portal, or contact the Patient Relations office (see the Contact Directory).

## Raising a concern early
Many concerns can be resolved quickly by talking to the team caring for you. Please tell staff as soon as possible so they can help.

## Making a formal complaint
If you wish to make a formal complaint, contact the Patient Relations office. You will be told how the process works, how long it may take, and who to contact for updates.

## It will not affect your care
Raising a concern or complaint will not negatively affect the care you receive.

## Support
You can ask a family member, friend, or an independent advocate to support you in raising a concern, and interpreter support is available.
