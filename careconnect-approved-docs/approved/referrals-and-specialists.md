# Referrals and Specialist Care (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## When you need a referral
Some specialist services, and some insurance plans, require a referral from your primary care provider before you can be seen. Your primary care team can arrange this.

## How referrals work
Once your primary care provider sends a referral, the specialist's office contacts you to arrange an appointment, or you can call them once the referral is on file. Referrals may have an expiry date.

## Checking referral status
You can check whether a referral has been received through the patient portal or by calling the specialist's office. The specialist usually cannot book you until the referral arrives.

## Insurance and referrals
If your plan requires a referral or pre-authorisation and it is missing, your insurer may not cover the visit. Confirm your plan's requirements with your insurer.

## Bringing information
For a specialist visit, bring relevant results, imaging, a medication list (names), and any referral paperwork so the specialist has what they need.
