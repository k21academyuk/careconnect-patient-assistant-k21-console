# Colonoscopy Preparation (Riverside Health — Approved Patient Leaflet, v2026-07)

Read these steps carefully. If anything is unclear, contact the scheduling desk. Follow any additional instructions your care team gives you, as they take priority over this general leaflet.

## Three days before
Avoid nuts, seeds, popcorn, and whole grains, which can leave residue.

## One day before
Follow a clear liquid diet only for the whole day. Clear liquids include water, clear broth, apple juice, and plain tea or coffee without milk. Do not drink anything coloured red or purple. Stop eating solid food after 12:00 (midday). Take the prescribed bowel-preparation solution the evening before, following the instructions printed on its label.

## The morning of the procedure
You may drink clear liquids up to two hours before your arrival time. After that, do not eat or drink anything.

## When you arrive
Arrive 30 minutes early. Bring your photo ID and insurance card.

## After the procedure
Because you may receive sedation, arrange for a responsible adult to drive you home. Do not drive, operate machinery, or sign legal documents for the rest of the day.

## Medication and clinical questions
This leaflet does not tell you whether to keep taking, pause, or change any medication (including blood thinners, diabetes medicines, or others) before the procedure. Those are clinical decisions made only by a licensed clinician. Contact your care team for advice about your specific medications.
