# Prescription Refill Policy (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Requesting a refill
You may request a refill when you have refills remaining on an active prescription. Check how many refills remain in the patient portal or on your pharmacy label.

## Review and approval
Every refill request is reviewed and approved by a licensed pharmacist before medication is dispensed. Requesting a refill does not guarantee it will be approved.

## Processing time
Standard refills are processed within 24–48 hours. Request refills before your current supply runs out.

## What refills cannot do
The refill process cannot change a medication, change a dose, or start a new medicine. Any change to a medication or dosage requires a licensed clinician and cannot be handled as a refill.

## No refills remaining
If no refills remain, the pharmacy may need to contact your clinician for a new authorisation, which can take extra time.

## Controlled medicines
Certain medicines have stricter rules and may not be refillable early or by phone. The pharmacy will explain what applies. See the Pharmacy Services guide.

## Medication and clinical questions
Questions about whether to take, stop, or change a medicine are clinical and are answered only by a licensed clinician, not through the refill process.
