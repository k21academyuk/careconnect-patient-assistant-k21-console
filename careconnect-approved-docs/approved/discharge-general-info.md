# Going Home After a Visit or Stay (General Discharge Information) (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

This is general information. Your personal discharge instructions from your care team always take priority.

## Your discharge summary
Before you leave, you will receive a discharge summary in the portal and/or on paper. It explains what happened during your visit and any follow-up arranged.

## Follow-up appointments
Any follow-up appointments or tests will be listed in your summary or arranged with you. You can view and manage them in the patient portal.

## Getting home
Arrange transport in advance, especially if you had sedation or surgery, as you may not be able to drive. See the relevant procedure leaflet for how long to avoid driving.

## Medicines at discharge
If you are given new prescriptions, collect them from the pharmacy before leaving where possible. Follow the specific written instructions provided with them.

## What the summary does not do
A discharge summary records your care and plan. It does not replace speaking with your clinician about symptoms or medication questions — those remain clinical decisions for your care team.

## If you feel unwell after going home
If you develop new or worsening symptoms after discharge, contact your care team using the number on your summary. For emergency symptoms, seek emergency care immediately.
