# Facilities, Parking, and Getting Around (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Parking
Visitor and patient parking is available in the multi-storey car park next to the main entrance. Accessible (disabled) parking bays are near each entrance. Bring your parking ticket for validation where offered.

## Drop-off
There is a short-stay drop-off zone outside the main entrance for setting down and picking up patients. Please do not leave vehicles unattended there.

## Finding your department
Colour-coded signs guide you from the main entrance to each department. Volunteers and reception staff can help with directions. Allow extra time to find your department for a first visit.

## Amenities
A café and vending machines are near the main lobby. Free visitor Wi-Fi is available — select the guest network and accept the terms. Cash machines are near the main entrance.

## Mobility support
Wheelchairs are available near the main entrance. If you need help moving through the building, ask at reception. See the Interpreter and Accessibility Services guide for more.

## Quiet and prayer spaces
A multi-faith prayer and quiet room is available. Ask reception for its location.
