# Billing FAQ (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## When will I get my bill?
You will receive an itemised statement within 14 days of your visit, listing services and any amount owed after insurance.

## Why might I get more than one bill?
You may receive separate bills from the facility and from individual providers (for example, a radiologist or anaesthetist). Each covers a different part of your care.

## How can I pay?
Pay online through the portal, by phone, or by mail. Have your statement or account number ready.

## Payment plans
Interest-free payment plans are available. Contact the billing office to set one up based on your balance.

## I think my bill is wrong
If you believe there is an error, contact the billing office with your statement number. Staff can review and explain the charges or correct a mistake.

## What is an Explanation of Benefits (EOB)?
An EOB comes from your insurer, not from Riverside Health. It shows what your insurer paid and what you may owe. It is not itself a bill.

## Financial help
Financial-assistance programs are available for qualifying patients. See the Financial Assistance guide.

## Insurance coverage questions
What your insurance did or did not cover is determined by your plan — contact your insurer for those questions.
