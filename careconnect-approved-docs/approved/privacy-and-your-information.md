# Privacy and How We Use Your Information (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

This is a patient-friendly summary of how Riverside Health handles your information. It is not legal advice.

## What we collect
We collect information needed for your care, such as your contact details, insurance information, and clinical records created during your visits.

## How we use it
Your information is used to provide and coordinate your care, to process billing and insurance, to meet legal and regulatory requirements, and to improve the quality and safety of care.

## Who can see it
Access is limited to people involved in your care and authorised staff, on a need-to-know basis. We do not sell your personal health information.

## Sharing with others
We share information with other providers or organisations only where allowed or required — for example, with your consent, for a referral, or as required by law.

## Your choices
You can request access to your records, ask for corrections, and set an authorisation for who may act on your behalf. See the Medical Records Request guide.

## Keeping data secure
Information is protected with administrative, physical, and technical safeguards, including access controls and encryption where appropriate.

## Questions
For privacy questions or to make a request, contact the Health Information office in the Contact Directory.
