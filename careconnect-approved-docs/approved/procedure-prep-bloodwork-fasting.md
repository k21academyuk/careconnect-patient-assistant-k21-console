# Fasting Blood Tests Preparation (Riverside Health — Approved Leaflet, v2026-07)

## Which tests need fasting
Some blood tests require fasting; many do not. Your appointment letter or the ordering clinician will tell you whether fasting is needed. Common fasting tests include certain glucose and cholesterol panels.

## How to fast
If fasting is required, do not eat or drink anything except plain water for the number of hours stated in your instructions (commonly 8–12 hours). Plain water is encouraged to stay hydrated and make the blood draw easier.

## Timing
Fasting appointments are often scheduled in the morning so you fast overnight. Bring a snack to eat immediately afterwards.

## Medicines
Do not stop or change any medication to prepare for a blood test unless a licensed clinician has specifically told you to. If you are unsure, contact your care team before the test.

## On the day
Bring your photo ID, insurance card, and the test request if you were given one. Tell the phlebotomist if you feel faint during blood draws so they can help you stay comfortable.
