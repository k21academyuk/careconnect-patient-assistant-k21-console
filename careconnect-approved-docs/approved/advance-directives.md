# Advance Directives (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## What an advance directive is
An advance directive is a document that records your wishes about your care if you are ever unable to speak for yourself. It may include a living will and the naming of a healthcare proxy (a person who can make decisions on your behalf).

## Why it helps
Having an advance directive helps your care team and family follow your wishes and reduces uncertainty during a difficult time.

## How to set one up
Ask reception, a social worker, or your care team for information and forms. You do not have to have one, and you can change or cancel it at any time.

## Sharing it with us
If you have an advance directive, please give a copy to Riverside Health so it can be added to your record. Tell your named proxy and family where to find the original.

## Questions
For help understanding the options, ask to speak with a social worker or patient advocate. Decisions about specific treatments are discussed with your clinician.
