# Insurance Basics (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Bring your card
Bring your current insurance card to every appointment so claims are filed correctly. Tell the front desk before your visit if your insurance has changed.

## Verifying coverage
Riverside Health can verify coverage before certain procedures. What you pay depends on your specific plan (deductible, co-pay, co-insurance, and out-of-pocket maximum), so confirm details with your insurer.

## Referrals and pre-authorisation
Some plans require a referral from your primary care provider or pre-authorisation before a procedure. Missing a required referral or authorisation can reduce or cancel coverage, so check with your insurer in advance.

## In-network vs out-of-network
Using in-network providers usually costs less. Ask whether the provider and facility are in your plan's network before a non-emergency procedure.

## If you are uninsured
If you do not have insurance, contact the billing office about self-pay pricing and financial-assistance programs. See the Financial Assistance guide.

## Questions
For questions about a specific claim or what your plan covers, contact your insurer directly. For questions about a Riverside Health bill, contact the billing office.
