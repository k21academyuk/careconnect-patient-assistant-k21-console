# MRI Scan Preparation (Riverside Health — Approved Leaflet, v2026-07)

An MRI uses a strong magnetic field, so safety screening is essential.

## Safety screening (very important)
Before your MRI you will complete a safety questionnaire. Tell the team in advance if you have any of the following, as they affect whether or how the scan is done: a pacemaker or defibrillator, cochlear implant, metal implants or clips, a metal fragment injury (for example, to the eye), or an implanted medication pump. If you are or may be pregnant, tell the team.

## What to wear and remove
Wear clothing without metal fasteners if possible. You will be asked to remove jewellery, watches, hairpins, hearing aids, and anything metal. Lockers are provided for valuables.

## Contrast
Some MRI scans use a contrast agent given through a small cannula. If your scan needs contrast, the team will explain it on the day. Tell them about any allergies or kidney problems.

## If you feel enclosed spaces are difficult
The scanner is an open-ended tube and can feel enclosing, and it is noisy — you will be offered ear protection. If enclosed spaces are difficult for you, tell the team when booking so support can be arranged.

## Duration and eating
Most scans take 15–60 minutes. You can usually eat and drink normally before an MRI unless your care team tells you otherwise.

## Medication and clinical questions
This leaflet does not give medication instructions. Any medication or clinical question is for your care team.
