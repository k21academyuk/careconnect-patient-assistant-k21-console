# Using the Patient Portal (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## What the portal does
The Riverside Health patient portal lets you book and manage appointments, view test results, request prescription refills, message your care team for non-urgent questions, view statements and pay bills, and update your contact details.

## Registering
To register, you need an activation code (given at a visit or by request) and a valid email address. Go to the Riverside Health portal, choose "Create account," and follow the steps to verify your identity.

## Signing in and resetting your password
Sign in with your email and password. If you forget your password, choose "Forgot password" to reset it by email. For your security, the portal may ask for a verification code.

## Mobile app
A mobile app is available for iOS and Android. Sign in with the same details as the website.

## Messaging your care team
Portal messages are for non-urgent questions and typically receive a reply within 1–2 business days. Do not use portal messages for emergencies — for urgent or emergency symptoms, seek urgent or emergency care.

## Trouble signing in
If you cannot sign in, use "Forgot password" first. If that does not help, contact the portal help desk listed in the Contact Directory.
