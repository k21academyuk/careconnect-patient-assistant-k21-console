# General Surgery Preparation (Pre-Operative) (Riverside Health — Approved Leaflet, v2026-07)

This general leaflet applies to many day-case and inpatient operations. Your surgical team will give you specific instructions that take priority.

## Fasting before surgery
You will usually be asked not to eat for several hours before surgery, and to stop clear fluids a shorter time before. Your exact fasting times will be on your admission instructions — follow them precisely, as eating or drinking too close to surgery can lead to your operation being postponed.

## Arranging your discharge
If you have an anaesthetic or sedation, you must arrange for a responsible adult to take you home and, for day surgery, to stay with you overnight. You should not drive for at least 24 hours, or longer if advised.

## What to bring
Bring your photo ID, insurance card, a list of your medications (names), a dressing gown and slippers, and any mobility aids. Leave jewellery and valuables at home.

## Skin, nails, and clothing
Remove nail polish and makeup. You may be asked to shower with a provided wash the night before and morning of surgery. Wear loose, comfortable clothing.

## Medication and clinical questions
Your surgical team will tell you which medications to take or hold before surgery. Do not make these decisions from this leaflet — always follow the clinician's specific instructions, and ask them if you are unsure.
