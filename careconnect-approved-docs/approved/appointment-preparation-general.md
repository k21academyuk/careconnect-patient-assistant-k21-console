# General Appointment Preparation (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

These are general steps for a routine appointment. Procedure-specific leaflets (for example, colonoscopy or surgery) add extra instructions.

## Before your appointment
Arrive 15 minutes early to check in. Bring your photo ID, insurance card, and a current list of your medications (names only — you do not need doses for a routine visit).

## What to bring
Bring any referral letters, results, or paperwork your care team asked for. If you use glasses, a hearing aid, or a mobility aid, bring them.

## Write down your questions
It helps to write down your questions and concerns before the visit so nothing is forgotten. You may bring a family member or friend for support.

## If you need to cancel
Give at least 24 hours' notice if you cannot attend. See the Rescheduling and Cancelling guide.

## Accessibility and language support
Tell the scheduling desk in advance if you need wheelchair access, an interpreter, or other support.

## Medication and clinical questions
This document does not tell you to start, stop, or change any medication. Any medication or clinical decision must come from a licensed clinician — please contact your care team.
