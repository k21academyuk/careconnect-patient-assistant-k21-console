# CT Scan (with or without Contrast) Preparation (Riverside Health — Approved Leaflet, v2026-07)

## Fasting
If your CT scan uses contrast, you may be asked not to eat for a few hours beforehand; you can usually still drink water. If your scan does not use contrast, fasting is often not required. Your appointment letter will state what applies to you.

## Tell the team about
Before a contrast scan, tell the team if you have any allergies (especially to contrast dye), kidney problems, diabetes, or if you are or may be pregnant. This information helps them keep the scan safe for you.

## Hydration
Drinking water before and after a contrast scan is often encouraged unless you have been told to limit fluids. Follow the specific advice in your appointment letter.

## What to wear
Wear comfortable clothing without metal fasteners. You may be asked to change into a gown and remove jewellery.

## Medication and clinical questions
Do not stop or change any medication based on this leaflet. If any medicine needs to be adjusted around a contrast scan, that is a clinical decision your care team will make and communicate to you directly.
