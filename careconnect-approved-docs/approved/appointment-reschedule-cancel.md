# Rescheduling and Cancelling Appointments (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Notice we ask for
Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule so the slot can be offered to another patient.

## How to reschedule or cancel
You can reschedule or cancel through the patient portal or by calling the scheduling desk during office hours. Have your appointment reference from your confirmation to hand.

## Missed appointments (no-shows)
If you miss an appointment without notice, it is recorded as a no-show. Repeated no-shows may affect how future appointments are booked. If you realise you will be late, call ahead — a short delay can sometimes still be accommodated.

## Late arrivals
If you arrive late, you may be asked to wait for the next available slot or to rebook, depending on the schedule that day.

## Procedures and specialist visits
Some procedures require preparation and booking of equipment or staff. Please give as much notice as possible for these so resources can be reassigned.

## Waitlist
Ask to join the cancellation waitlist through the portal if you would like to be offered an earlier appointment when one becomes available.
