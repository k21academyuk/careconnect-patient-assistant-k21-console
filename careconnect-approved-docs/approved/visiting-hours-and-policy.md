# Visiting Hours and Visitor Policy (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## General wards
General ward visiting hours are 11:00–20:00 every day, including weekends and public holidays. A maximum of two visitors are allowed at a bedside at one time.

## Intensive Care Unit (ICU)
ICU visiting is limited to two named visitors per patient. Visits may be shorter and can be adjusted by the care team based on the patient's condition. Please use the intercom at the ICU entrance and wait to be admitted.

## Maternity and birthing
A birth partner may stay at any time. Other visitors follow 11:00–20:00 hours. To protect newborns, visitors who are unwell should not attend.

## Children's ward (paediatrics)
A parent or guardian may stay with a child at any time, including overnight. Other visitors follow general ward hours.

## Surgical recovery
Patients in recovery are usually seen briefly by one visitor once the care team confirms they are ready. Staff will let you know when you can go in.

## Infection prevention
During flu season or an outbreak, visiting may be restricted and masks may be required. Please check current notices at the main entrance or on the Riverside Health website before travelling.

## Please do not visit if you are unwell
Do not visit if you have a fever, cough, sore throat, vomiting, diarrhoea, rash, or any contagious illness. This protects patients whose immune systems may be weakened.

## Flowers, food, and gifts
Fresh flowers are not permitted in some wards (including ICU) for infection-control reasons. Please check with ward staff before bringing food for a patient, as some patients have dietary restrictions.

## Check in
Please check in at the front desk on arrival and wear a visitor badge for the duration of your visit.
