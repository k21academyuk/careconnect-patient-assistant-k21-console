# Requesting Your Medical Records (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## What you can request
You can request a copy of your medical records, including visit summaries, test results, imaging reports, and discharge documents.

## How to request
Submit a records request through the patient portal or by completing a records-request form at the Health Information/Medical Records office. You will need to verify your identity.

## Timelines
Records requests are usually completed within a set number of business days, depending on the size of the request and the format you choose (electronic or paper). You will be told the expected timeframe when you submit.

## Records for another person
To request records for someone else — such as a child, or an adult you are authorised to act for — you must provide proof of your authority (for example, guardianship or a signed authorisation). Without valid authorisation, records cannot be released.

## Sending records to a third party
To have records sent to another provider, insurer, or organisation, complete an authorisation-to-release form specifying exactly what may be shared and with whom. You can withdraw this authorisation in writing at any time for future disclosures.

## Fees
Some records requests may involve a reasonable fee, especially for large or paper copies. Any fee will be explained before the request is processed.
