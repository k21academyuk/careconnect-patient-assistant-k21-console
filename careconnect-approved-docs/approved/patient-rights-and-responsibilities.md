# Patient Rights and Responsibilities (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Your rights
You have the right to be treated with respect and dignity; to privacy and confidentiality of your health information; to receive information about your care in a way you understand; to ask questions and seek a second opinion; to know who is involved in your care; and to give or withhold consent for treatment.

## Interpreters and support
You have the right to an interpreter and to reasonable adjustments for a disability, at no cost to you.

## Access to records
You have the right to access your medical records and to request corrections. See the Medical Records Request guide.

## Your responsibilities
Please provide accurate information about your health and insurance; treat staff and other patients with respect; attend appointments or give notice if you cannot; follow safety rules on hospital premises; and ask if you do not understand something about your care.

## Raising concerns
You have the right to raise a concern or complaint without it affecting your care. See the Feedback and Complaints guide.
