# Emergency and Urgent Care Information (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

This document explains where to go for different levels of care. It is general information and does not replace advice from a clinician.

## In an emergency — call emergency services
For any life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number (for example, 911) or go to the nearest Emergency Department immediately. General warning signs that need emergency care include: chest pain or pressure, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding that will not stop, sudden weakness, numbness, or difficulty speaking, a serious injury, a severe allergic reaction, or if someone is unresponsive.

## Emergency Department
The Riverside Health Emergency Department is open 24 hours a day, every day, at the main hospital site. It is for serious and life-threatening conditions.

## Urgent care
Urgent care is for problems that are not life-threatening but need attention the same day (for example, minor injuries or mild illnesses). Urgent care hours and locations are listed on the Riverside Health website and in the Contact Directory.

## Primary care
For routine and ongoing health needs, book with your primary care provider through the portal or scheduling desk.

## If you are unsure
If you are unsure how serious a symptom is, treat it as urgent and seek care promptly. The patient-support assistant cannot assess how urgent a symptom is — that is a clinical judgement — so it will direct clinical or urgent questions to a licensed clinician or to emergency services.
