# Cost Estimates Before Care (Riverside Health — Approved, v2026-07)

## Getting an estimate
You can ask for a good-faith cost estimate before a scheduled, non-emergency service. Contact the billing office or use the estimate tool in the patient portal where available.

## What affects the estimate
An estimate depends on the specific service, your insurance plan, whether providers are in-network, and whether additional services are needed during care. The final bill may differ if your care changes.

## Uninsured and self-pay
If you are paying yourself, ask for the self-pay price and about financial assistance. See the Financial Assistance guide.

## Insurance estimates
For the most accurate out-of-pocket estimate, check with your insurer, since your deductible and benefits determine what you owe.

## Surprise billing protections
For certain emergency services and some care from out-of-network providers at an in-network facility, protections may limit unexpected charges. The billing office can explain how these apply to your situation.
